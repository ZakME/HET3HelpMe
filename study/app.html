Mock AIT period exams

Each full mock is 100 four-option questions weighted like the real AIT exam (which is about 120 questions): 50 from 301 Hydraulics, 32 from 303 Powertrain and 18 from 302 Steering/Suspension/Electrical. There is no feedback until you submit, like the real thing. Mocks 1–6 are fixed sets, so a retake shows the same questions and you can measure improvement. Each one pulls different questions from the bank where it can. Aim for 70% or better. Check the pass mark and time limit for your actual exam with your institution or AIT.
