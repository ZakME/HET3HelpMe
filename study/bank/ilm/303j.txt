Q: Which of these is NOT a function of an off-road drive axle?
+ Allowing speed differences between the front and rear axles
- Supporting the gross vehicle weight
- Turning the path of power 90 degrees to the wheel ends
- Providing a final gear reduction

E: The sum of the axle ratings = the maximum weight rating for the unit's design and application.
