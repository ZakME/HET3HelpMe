Q: Steering lever NOT pulled on a conventional steer system. Where does the steering pump oil go?
+ Over the steering relief valve, through the cooler, to lube
- It is sent to the clutch piston to keep the clutch engaged
- It is sent to the brake booster to hold the brakes released
- It returns directly to the reservoir through the open-centre spool
